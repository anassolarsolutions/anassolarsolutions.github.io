Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about solar and electrical services

What is the cost of a complete solar system in Pakistan?
The price depends on system size, panels, inverter, batteries and installation. Contact us for a current quotation.
How much can I save with solar?
Savings depend on your electricity usage, system size, tariff and grid/net-metering arrangements.
Do solar panels work during load shedding?
Hybrid or off-grid systems with suitable battery backup can supply selected loads during outages.
What maintenance do solar panels require?
Regular cleaning and periodic inspection of panels, wiring, protection devices and mounting are recommended.
